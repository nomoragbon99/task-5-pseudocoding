================================================================================
PART A1: OWN FUNCTION 3 - EXPONENTIAL BACKOFF WITH EQUAL JITTER
================================================================================

FUNCTION calculateExponentialBackoffWithJitter
INPUTS:
  - attempt: Integer, the retry attempt index (starting from 1)
  - baseMs: Number, base retry delay duration in milliseconds
  - capMs: Number, maximum ceiling limit for delay duration in milliseconds
  - randomFraction: Number, pseudo-random float value between 0.0 inclusive and 1.0 exclusive
OUTPUT:
  - delayMs: Integer, computed backoff delay duration in milliseconds
SIDE EFFECTS:
  - NONE
FAILS WHEN:
  - attempt is not a whole number or is less than 1
  - baseMs is not a positive number or is not finite
  - capMs is less than baseMs or is not finite
  - randomFraction is less than 0.0 or greater than or equal to 1.0

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PSEUDOCODE BODY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Validate the retry attempt counter.
   IF attempt is not a whole number OR attempt is less than 1 THEN
     RAISE ERROR "Attempt must be an integer greater than or equal to 1"
   OTHERWISE continue.

2. Validate the base backoff duration.
   IF baseMs is not a valid number OR baseMs is less than or equal to 0 THEN
     RAISE ERROR "baseMs must be greater than 0"
   OTHERWISE continue.

3. Validate the cap duration against the base duration.
   IF capMs is not a valid number OR capMs is less than baseMs THEN
     RAISE ERROR "capMs must be greater than or equal to baseMs"
   OTHERWISE continue.

4. Validate the pseudo-random factor.
   IF randomFraction is less than 0.0 OR randomFraction is greater than or equal to 1.0 THEN
     RAISE ERROR "randomFraction must be between 0 (inclusive) and 1 (exclusive)"
   OTHERWISE continue.

5. Compute the exponential scaling multiplier by raising the base number 2 to the power of (attempt minus 1).
   Store the result as multiplier.

6. Compute the uncapped exponential duration by multiplying baseMs by multiplier.
   Store the result as uncappedDelay.

7. Bound the exponential duration by selecting the smaller value between capMs and uncappedDelay.
   Store the result as exponentialDelay.

8. Determine the deterministic half of the bounded duration by dividing exponentialDelay by 2.
   Store the result as halfDelay.

9. Determine the random jitter portion by multiplying halfDelay by randomFraction.
   Store the result as jitterPortion.

10. Combine the deterministic half and the jitter portion to obtain the total raw delay.
    Store the sum of halfDelay and jitterPortion as totalDelay.

11. Round totalDelay to the nearest whole integer.
    Store the result as delayMs.

12. RETURN delayMs.
