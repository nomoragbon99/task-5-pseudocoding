================================================================================
PART A1: OWN FUNCTION 1 - ATOMIC JOB CLAIM ROUTINE (SKIP LOCKED)
================================================================================

FUNCTION claimNextJobs
INPUTS:
  - workerId: String, unique identifier of the worker claiming the jobs
  - batchSize: Integer, maximum number of due jobs to claim in this batch
  - queue: List of Job Records, the database table or in-memory job queue
  - currentTime: Timestamp, the current reference time for determining job eligibility
OUTPUT:
  - claimedJobs: List of Job Records, the jobs claimed and transitioned to processing status
SIDE EFFECTS:
  - WRITE updates the status of claimed jobs from pending or failed to processing
  - WRITE updates the lockedBy field of claimed jobs to workerId
  - WRITE updates the startedAt timestamp of claimed jobs to currentTime
FAILS WHEN:
  - workerId is empty or contains only whitespace
  - batchSize is not an integer or is less than 1
  - Database connection fails during query execution (CALL database query (may fail, may be slow))

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PSEUDOCODE BODY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Validate the worker identifier parameter.
   IF workerId is not text OR workerId contains only blank spaces THEN
     RAISE ERROR "workerId must be a non-empty string"
   OTHERWISE continue.

2. Validate the requested batch size.
   IF batchSize is not a whole number OR batchSize is less than 1 THEN
     RAISE ERROR "batchSize must be a positive integer"
   OTHERWISE continue.

3. Initialize an empty list named candidateJobs to hold runnable jobs.

4. Scan the job queue to find all unlocked jobs that are due for execution.
   CALL database query (may fail, may be slow) to fetch rows matching criteria:
   FOR EACH job IN queue:
     IF (job status is "pending" OR job status is "failed")
        AND job runAt is before or equal to currentTime
        AND job lockedBy is empty THEN
       APPEND job TO candidateJobs
     OTHERWISE continue.
   END FOR

5. Sort candidateJobs in ascending order of their scheduled execution time runAt so that the oldest overdue jobs are prioritized.

6. Determine the number of jobs to take as the smaller value between batchSize and the total count of candidateJobs.

7. Slice candidateJobs from the beginning up to the determined count, naming the resulting collection selectedJobs.

8. Initialize an empty list named claimedJobs to store the updated records.

9. Atomically mark each selected job as locked by this worker.
   FOR EACH job IN selectedJobs:
     WRITE job status to "processing"
     WRITE job lockedBy to workerId
     WRITE job startedAt to currentTime
     APPEND a copy of job TO claimedJobs
   END FOR

10. RETURN claimedJobs.
